# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and
focus on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption. Maintainers may
remove or reject contributions and ban participants for inappropriate behavior.

Report conduct issues privately through the repository owner's contact channel.
Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
